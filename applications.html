📖 Official Application Procedures & Step-by-Step OTR Guidance (Feature 34)

Standard Procedural Codes
UPSC Portal

UPSC One-Time Registration (OTR)

  1. Visit upsconline.nic.in and click "New Registration (OTR)".
  2. Fill basic demographic details matching your 10th Board Certificate exactly.
  3. Verify Email ID and Mobile number using Dual OTP authentication.
  4. Set security questions and login password.
  5. Note down your permanent OTR ID. This is valid for all future UPSC notifications (CSE, CDS, NDA, ESE).
SSC Portal

SSC New Portal (ssc.gov.in) One-Time Registration

  1. Visit the upgraded SSC portal at ssc.gov.in.
  2. Click "Register Now" and enter personal details & Aadhaar number.
  3. Create your new Registration Number and 6-digit Password.
  4. Live Photo Capture: SSC now captures photo via device webcam. Ensure plain white background, ample natural lighting, and remove spectacles/caps.
  5. Upload running signature (10 to 20 KB in JPEG format).
IBPS & State PSC

IBPS / SBI / State PSC Application Tips

  1. Left Thumb Impression (LTI): Ensure clear blue/black ink on plain white sheet (20-50 KB).
  2. Handwritten Declaration: Must be written in candidate's own handwriting in English on plain white paper. Capital letters are strictly rejected.
  3. Payment Modes: UPI, Debit Card, Internet Banking. Always preserve the E-Receipt printout.

🚨 Official Exam-Day Checklist & Venue Guidelines (Feature 37)

Strict Enforcement Protocol
Gate Closure Warning: In UPSC, SSC, and Banking examinations, the examination venue gates close strictly 30 minutes before commencement (e.g. 08:30 AM for a 09:00 AM session). No candidate is admitted under any circumstances after gate closure. Plan to arrive at least 60–90 minutes in advance.
