        Your demo data

        Profile, preferences, study activity, checklists and application records are stored in localStorage. This is not a secure account system and does not sync across devices.

        Help & service status

        Are notifications and deadlines live?

        No. Listings are sample records. Check the linked commission portal directly for current dates and notices.

        Does this page read my PDF?

        No. Selecting a file only displays its name and size. PDF extraction requires a document service that is not configured.

        Does the career assistant use AI?

        No. It shows a local demonstration response. No prompt is sent to an AI service.

        Where is my data stored?

        In this browser's localStorage. Do not save passwords, identity documents, or sensitive personal information in this prototype.
